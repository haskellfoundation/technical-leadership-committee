## Motivation and purpose

The initiative to form this committee comes from the Haskell Board itself. The [technical leadership document](https://gitlab.haskell.org/hf/meta/-/blob/main/committees/technical-leadership.md?ref_type=heads)
describes the structure and bylaws.

In short, our mission is to:

- consolidate the technical needs of both the community and industry
- spearhead initiatives to drive Haskell forward and provide guidance on technical directions
- decide on funding of technical proposals

## Community proposal process

While this committee is a proactive force that actively looks for passionate Haskellers who can implement
our agenda, members of the community can also appeal themselves and ask for funding directly. They can do
so through whichever [channel](#contact) they prefer.

A proposal does not need to follow a specific structure or template, but should convey a clear motivation,
outline a concrete and achievable goal and be specific about the required funding and timeline.

Every proposal requires a "sponsor" from the committee who will be the primary contact during the process
and implementation.

## Ideas, discussions, referrals

Members of the community are also free to reach out and express their ideas and concerns or refer
trustworthy people who can contract for the Haskell Foundation.
